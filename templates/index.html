Comprehensive Guide to Nepal Vehicle License Plates

Understand the rules, zonal formats, provincial standards, and embossed coding system in Nepal.

01

Traditional Zonal Plates (Devanagari)

बा ९२ प ५६७८

Used widely across Nepal prior to provincial federalization. Identified by an administrative zone abbreviation:

  • बा (BA): Bagmati Zone (Kathmandu Valley)
  • ना (NA): Narayani Zone (Chitwan, Birgunj)
  • ९२ (92): Lot / Series number
  • प (PA): Vehicle category symbol (Two-Wheeler)
  • ५६७८: 4-digit vehicle registration number
02

Traditional Provincial Plates (Devanagari)

बा.प्र. ०२-०३५ प ५६७८

Adopted after Nepal transitioned to federal provinces:

  • बा.प्र. / बाप्र: बागमती प्रदेश (Province 3)
  • ०२-०३५: District code & Lot sequence number
  • प: Vehicle category symbol
  • ५६७८: 4-digit unique registration number
03

High-Security Embossed Plates (Latin Script)

BAGMATI PROVINCE
02-035 PA 5678

Modern national standard with laser-etched barcodes and RFID chips:

  • BAGMATI PROVINCE: Province name header
  • 02-035: Province code & series
  • PA, PB, PC: Vehicle category classification
  • 5678: 4-digit vehicle number
